Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust are important to us. Any personal information you provide—including your name, email address, contact details, uploaded files, and project information—is used solely for communicating with you and completing your project.

All client files, ideas, and project details are treated as confidential and will never be shared with third parties without your permission, unless required by applicable law. We take reasonable measures to protect your information and handle every project responsibly.

However, while giving material we expect that you will not violate any copyright or other IP laws and you will be fully responsible for the content.

Usage Rights

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, all final deliverables provided to you are supplied without our branding or watermarks, allowing you to use them for your intended personal or commercial purposes. Once your project is completed and delivered, you are free to present the final work as part of your brand or business in accordance with the agreed project terms.

If a project includes third-party assets (such as stock images, fonts, music, or licensed materials), their use remains subject to the original creator's license. We will never knowingly include copyrighted material in your project without the appropriate rights or permissions.

Furthermore, we will use your name in our client list, but if you do not want to be part of that list you can expressly say so.
